I am a member of the Signal Processing and Deep Learning laboratory, advised by [Dr. Akshay Rangamani](https://akshay-r.github.io/). Research Overview
======

Generally, I am interested in all things optimization and statistics. My recent research lies in using machine learning methods to solve applied tasks for human-in-the-loop systems, and understanding deep learning models through the field of Mechanistic Interpretability.
